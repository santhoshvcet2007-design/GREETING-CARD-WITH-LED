  
                       **LED GREETING CARD** 

* Aim

      We made a creative greeting card using LEDs and simple electrical connections. The LED light makes the card more attractive and beautiful.

*  Materials Required  
* Chart paper  
* Colour papers  
* LEDs  
* Battery  
* Connecting wires  
* Aluminium foil  
* Tape / glue  
* Scissors  
* Sketch pens  
* Decorative materials

 

* Step-by-Step Procedure  
    
- Step 1   Preparing the Card

  First, we took a thick chart paper and folded it to make a greeting card.

- Step 2  – Making the Design

  We decided on a simple heart and flower design for the card.

- Step 3  – Making the Hearts

  We cut pink colour paper into heart shapes and used black paper as the outline. Then, we pasted the hearts on the card.

- Step 4  – Making the Stems and Leaves

  We cut green colour paper into thin strips and leaf shapes. We pasted them below the hearts to make them look like flowers.

- Step 5  – Adding Decoration

  We drew small stars around the design using sketch pens to make the card more attractive.

- Step 6  – LED Connection  
  We connected the LED with the battery using connecting wires. The LED was placed near the greeting card design.![Circuits](Circuits.png
)
- Step 7 –  Making the Conductive Strip  
  We used aluminium foil as a conducting material and fixed it properly with tape.  
- Step 8 –  Testing the Circuit  
  We checked the connections and switched ON the circuit. The LED started glowing when the connection was completed.  
- Step 9 –  Final Arrangement

Finally, we arranged and fixed all the wires neatly and completed the decoration.

*  Working

When the circuit is connected to the battery, electric current flows through the conducting path and the LED. As a result, the LED glows and gives a beautiful lighting effect to the greeting card.

*  Result  
  We successfully made a creative LED greeting card using simple materials and an LED circuit.
